    Async

    The tasks a running service holds, the way one delivery passes through them, and the order a shutdown stops them in.

    The service runs on Tokio. #[ruststream::app] builds a multi-thread runtime. flavor = "current_thread" picks a single-threaded one, and worker_threads = n sets the number of threads.

    A subscriber is a Stream of deliveries, and back-pressure comes from polling it. The default loop polls for the next delivery once the previous one is settled. workers(n) keeps at most n deliveries in flight. workers(n, by_key) sends every delivery of one key to the same worker, so one key keeps its order. threads(n) reads ahead at most n × 9 deliveries: one in the hands of each thread and eight in its ring.

    A dedicated thread runs the whole delivery, from decode to settlement, on its own current-thread runtime. Work the handler hands to MainRuntime, taken as Ctx<MainRuntime>, runs back on the app runtime.

    One delivery​

    The handling task is the loop task itself by default, a worker task under workers(n), and a dedicated thread under threads(n). An and_after continuation, a post-settle hook and a retry_after timer run after the settlement, as tasks the app tracks. They run at most once: the delivery is already settled.

    Shutdown​

    A shutdown timeout bounds the wait. When it expires, loops still running are aborted, and tracked tasks still pending are left to finish on their own. Without a timeout, a shutdown waits for all of them. A task spawned with plain tokio::spawn on a dedicated thread is not tracked and ends with its thread.
